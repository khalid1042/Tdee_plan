### Template 5: Cronometer Community (Forum Value Post)
**Platform:** [Cronometer Discussion Thread #7331](https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/7331/tdee-calculators-are-scam-and-do-not-work-dont-bother-with-them-heres-far-more-accurate-method)  
**Message Concept:**

> Completely agree with the core point here. Formula calculators (Mifflin-St Jeor, Harris-Benedict) are strictly population averages based on indirect calorimetry in controlled cohorts. They cannot see your non-exercise activity (NEAT), sleep quality, or digestive efficiency.
> 
> The biggest trap people fall into is adjusting calories based on day-to-day scale changes. Because 1g of stored muscle glycogen binds approximately 3–4g of water, a single high-carb or salty meal can swing the scale 2–4 lbs without a single gram of fat change.
> 
> Comparing the rolling average of your first 7 days against your next 7–14 days filters out that hydration noise. If anyone wants a free browser-based tool to do that math without setting up complex Excel sheets, we put together an open, client-side calculator here: https://tdeecalculater.com/calibrate-tdee-weight-trends/ (it saves everything locally in your browser storage, no account or data sent anywhere).
