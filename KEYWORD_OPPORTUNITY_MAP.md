# TDEECalculator.com — Keyword Opportunity Map & Long-Tail SEO Strategy

**Project:** TDEECalculator.com  
**Website:** `https://tdeecalculater.com/`  
**Target Market:** USA first, followed by global English-speaking audiences  
**Approach:** Search Intent Mapping · Differentiated Value · Zero Keyword Stuffing  

---

## 1. Strategy Overview

Rather than mass-producing thin landing pages targeting arbitrary keyword permutations, TDEECalculator.com follows a **hub-and-spoke topical authority architecture**:
1. **Primary Hub (The Homepage `/`):** Owns the head term **"TDEE Calculator"**. Serves broad discovery, immediate calculation, and general education.
2. **Specialized Calculator Spokes (Tools):** Own specific mathematical intents (BMR, Maintenance Calories, Calorie Deficit, Calorie Surplus / Lean Bulk, Macros, Calories Burned).
3. **Intent-Driven Guide Spokes (Articles):** Answer deep, specific informational questions that cannot be addressed in a single calculator widget (e.g., adaptive trend tracking, formula comparisons, training day splits).

## 3. Prioritization Scoring & Evaluation Framework

Each long-tail query is scored across 5 criteria (1–5 scale, max 25 points):
1. **Relevance to Core Product (1–5):** Direct applicability to the calculator suite.
2. **Search Demand Evidence (1–5):** Verified intent and continuous query volume.
3. **Competitive SERP Feasibility (1–5):** Ability to out-rank incumbent generic content with deep technical rigor and interactive tools.
4. **Information Gain & Differentiation Potential (1–5):** Ability to provide unique tools (Wizard, Lab, Diagnostics) rather than generic text.
5. **Conversion to Active Calculator Usage (1–5):** Likelihood that the searcher will engage with our interactive tools.

### Prioritization Rankings:
1. **Movement Questionnaire for Physical Jobs & Daily Steps** (Score: **24/25**) → **Immediate Implementation** on Homepage `/`.
2. **Adaptive TDEE Calibration Lab & Trend Tracking** (Score: **24/25**) → **Immediate Enhancement** on Homepage `/#calibration-lab`.
3. **BMR vs TDEE vs Maintenance Calories Intent Clarification** (Score: **23/25**) → **Maintain Specialized Silos** (`/bmr-calculator/`, `/maintenance-calorie-calculator/`, `/blog/tdee-vs-bmr/`).
4. **Wearable & Step Count Integration Guidance** (Score: **21/25**) → **Incorporate into `/calories-burned-calculator/` and Homepage Wizard**.
5. **Calorie Deficit & Macro Allocation for Fat Loss** (Score: **21/25**) → **Maintain `/calorie-deficit-calculator/` & `/macro-calculator/`**.

## 4. Cannibalization Prevention Rules

To safeguard topical authority and avoid self-competing pages:
- **Rule 1: Homepage `/` owns "TDEE Calculator".** No `/tdee-calculator/`, `/free-tdee-calculator/`, or `/online-tdee-calculator/` URLs shall ever be generated.
- **Rule 2: One Primary Search Intent Per Canonical URL.** Informational guides must not attempt to duplicate the primary calculator, and calculators must not pad pages with redundant 3,000-word generic definitions.
- **Rule 3: Clean Canonical Mapping.** Subpage aliases must redirect with HTTP 301 rather than generating self-canonical duplicate static pages.
